How a duel works

Five steps, all verified on-chain.

  1. 1Accredit

    The contract draws 20 problems and scores you itself. ELO starts at 800–1800.

  2. 2Queue

    Matched within an ELO band that widens with wait time (±150 → ±450). Equal stakes.

  3. 3Commit

    You have 30s. Submit a hash of your answer, bound to the duel and your account.

  4. 4Reveal

    20s to reveal. The contract verifies integer / modular math across 8 problem kinds.

  5. 5Settle

    First correct commit wins the pot minus 3% rake (60% treasury / 40% burn). Draws are refunded minus rake.
